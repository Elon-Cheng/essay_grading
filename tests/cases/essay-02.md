## Essay

To the school committee,

I’m Li Hua from Mingqi High School. Words came that our school is gathering students’ intention on new clubs. As a Hanfu enthusiast, I’m writing to ask for your permission to establish Hanfu Club.

Nowadays, Hanfu is becoming more and more popular with the revive of Chinese traditional culture. Hanfu Club can serve as a platform for students and teachers to deepen their understanding of Hanfu and Hanfu culture. The etiquette behind Hanfu can inspire students to behave in a polite way. What’s more, while learning more about Hanfu, students’ interest in long-lasting and profound Chinese traditional culture will also be aroused.

Our club would like to hold two activities in the next school term. The first one is a Hanfu exhibition. Our club members will exhibit different kinds of Hanfu from different periods and introduce their style and history. Students can relax from busy studying and appreciate the beauty of Hanfu closely. They can also gain more knowledge of Hanfu culture.

The other activity is an adult ceremony for Senior Three students. In ancient times, a solemn ceremony called Guan Li, would be held when a person became an adult. I think we should pass down this tradition. On the ceremony, students will dress in Hanfu and elders will place a cap on their head formally, which will remind them of their responsibilities and duties as an adult.

I hope you will approve my proposal for the club.

Yours sincerely,
Li Hua
