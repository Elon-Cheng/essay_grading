## Essay

To whom it may concern:

Greetings! Hearing that the school is setting up new clubs, I am writing to put forward my proposal of setting up the Hanfu club. My aims and primary designs are as follows.

To gather kindred spirits of Hanfu lovers and provide a platform for sharing one's insights on Hanfu is the principal aim of the club. It also sets out to educate the students on the eclectic mix of customs and culture of Hanfu, therefore imparting the Confucian values underlying the Hanfu culture.

As for its design, I conceive of several activities in mind. First, with the members all dressed in the iconic Hanfus of their favourite Han characters or celebraties, a cosplay display can be held to break the ice and form a mutual understanding of our zest for Hanfu, since so many of the Hanfu lovers start as outsiders mesmerized by a particular Han story or historical figure.

Apart from that, the members are encouraged to make their own Hanfus from scratch as well. This is a challenge that develops integrative skills such as design and sewing, encompassing various fields from aesthetics to the intricate dressing code of Hanfu. Furthermore, members shall gain a great sense of achievement that will fuel their passion for future studies in Hanfu.

Finally, the members can hold a lecture to the school about Hanfu. Not only can it deepens the members' own understanding as they strive to give a comprehensive introduction of Hanfu, but it also promotes this elegant clothing to the general audience through intricating facts and enlightening knowledge.

Conclusively, the Hanfu club will certainly give the school a spiritual uplift and benefits its members tremendously. I hope you can take my proposal into consideration.

Yours sincerely,
Lihua
